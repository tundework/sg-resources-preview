Checklist

Exporting a car from the U.S.

What U.S. Customs needs before a used vehicle can leave the country, and what happens when it arrives. We'll help you get each document ready.

Before it leaves · U.S. requirements

Documents & filings

  • Original certificate of titleOr a certified copy, plus two copies. U.S. Customs requires the title for every vehicle export, whatever its value or condition.

    Source: U.S. Customs and Border Protection

  • Lender or leasing company's permission, if there's a loan or leaseA letter saying the vehicle may be exported. We handle lien releases.

    Source: CBP

  • Presented to Customs at least 72 hours before it sailsFor sea and air exports, the documents and the vehicle must both reach Customs at least 72 hours before export. Plan around this deadline.

    Source: CBP

  • Electronic Export Information (EEI) filed in AESRequired for every used vehicle, whatever its value or destination, and also due 72 hours before export. Ask us how it's filed for your shipment.

    Source: CBP, 15 CFR 30.2

  • Usually also needed: bill of sale and your photo IDMost carriers and terminals ask for these too.

When it arrives · destination

Duties & clearance

  • Import duties and port chargesThese are paid in the destination country, not in the U.S.
  • A licensed clearing agentThe agent clears the vehicle through customs. We help with the shipping documents and TELEX release.
  • Check the destination's rules before you buyAge limits, banned items and required documents are set by each country and change from time to time. Ask us for the current rules for your port.

General information, not legal advice. U.S. requirements last checked September 2026. We don't insure vehicles in transit; see our shipping & liability policy.

FAQ

Vehicle questions

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

My car still has a loan on it. Can I export it?

Only with written permission from the lender. U.S. Customs requires it. We can handle the lien release for you.

Can you find a car for me to buy?

Yes. Our dealership sources vehicles for buyers in Africa and overseas, and can title and ship them for you.

Can I ship a car and goods together?

Yes, in a shared (consolidated) container. What you can pack inside a vehicle depends on the carrier and the destination, so check with us first.

Is my vehicle insured while it ships?

Not by us. We don't provide insurance, so arrange coverage through your own insurance provider if you want it.
