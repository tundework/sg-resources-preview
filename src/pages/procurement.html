What we source

Tell us what you need

Food service equipment, auto parts, furniture, building supplies: if you need it bought and shipped, send us the details.

Buying from overseas is risky when you can't see the goods or meet the seller. We find the suppliers, negotiate the price, and vet and verify every transaction, which takes the uncertainty out of buying.

Once the goods are bought, we can store them in Houston and ship them with the rest of your cargo, all through one company.

How it works

From request to shipment

You deal with one team from the first request until the goods ship.

  1. Request

    Send us what you need: item, quantity, specs, budget and where it's going.

  2. Source

    We find suppliers and negotiate on price and terms.

  3. Verify

    We vet the supplier and verify the transaction.

  4. Store & ship

    Goods can be held in Houston and shipped with your other cargo.
