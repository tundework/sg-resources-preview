What we do

From Houston, every week

We run air and sea freight services every week to Lagos and Onne in Nigeria, Accra in Ghana, Dakar in Senegal and Douala in Cameroon, and to other destinations on request.

We know the customs process, deal directly with vendors, and keep track of your cargo at every step, so exporting stays clear and predictable. We handle full containers, consolidated containers, roll-on/roll-off, flat racks and expedited air cargo.

Getting a quote

How it works

Send as much detail as you can up front. It makes the first quote more accurate.

  1. Tell us

    What you're shipping, roughly how big and heavy it is, where it's going and when it will be ready.

  2. Get a quote

    We recommend a shipping method and send you a price and the expected dates.

  3. Hand over

    We'll tell you where to deliver your cargo, or arrange door-to-port pickup.

  4. Ship & release

    Your cargo sails or flies, and we help with clearance and TELEX release at the destination.

We don't provide insurance for your goods. To insure your shipment, get coverage through your own private insurance provider. Read our shipping & liability policy before you ship.
