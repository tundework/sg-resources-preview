Accessibility statement

SG Resources Inc wants this website to be easy to use for everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, so the site works with assistive technology such as screen readers, keyboard navigation and voice control.

What we've done

  • Images have text descriptions.
  • Text and buttons have strong color contrast.
  • Every link, button and form field can be reached with a keyboard, and shows a visible focus outline.
  • Pages use headings and labels that screen readers can follow.
  • Motion is kept to a minimum and turned off if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages work when zoomed in and on small phone screens.

Tell us about problems

If something on this site is hard to use, please tell us and we'll work to fix it. We can also give you the information in another way.
