Services

Collect, store, load

Buying from several U.S. sellers? Have everything delivered to us, and we'll check it, store it and load it into one container.

Our Houston-based warehouse handles short-term storage while your cargo waits to ship, plus packing, labeling and inventory tracking so you know what's there.

Houston office hours: Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm. Saturday by appointment only. Contact us before sending deliveries so we can expect them.

In the warehouse

  • Cross-docking and container stuffing
  • Short-term storage
  • Fulfillment, packing and labeling
  • Inventory management and asset tracking

Typical use

Consolidating a shipment

This is the most common way customers use our warehouse together with our freight service.

  1. Buy

    You buy from U.S. stores or sellers, or we source the goods for you.

  2. Deliver

    Everything is delivered to our Houston warehouse.

  3. Check & store

    We receive, label and track each item until the shipment is complete.

  4. Load & ship

    Everything goes into one container, shipped to your port.

We don't insure goods in storage or in transit. See our shipping & liability policy.
