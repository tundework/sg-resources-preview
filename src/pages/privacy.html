Privacy policy

How this website handles the information you send us.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. When you fill in the quote form, we receive your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (if you give it), the service you're asking about, your destination and the details you type.

The form's information reaches our company email (info@sgresourcesinc.com) through a form-delivery service (Formspree) or through your own email app. If you contact us on WhatsApp, your messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and prepare a quote.
  • To provide the services you ask for, and to keep records of them.

We don't sell your personal information.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security purposes.

Links to other sites

We link to sites such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps and U.S. government websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email info@sgresourcesinc.com or write to us at 2638 5th St., Ste. 15, Stafford, TX 77477.
