    We do this

    We do this

Redesign notes

Version 3: this version uses the real logo artwork and adds logistics photos. The colors match the logo, and the copy sticks to what the company has stated publicly.

About the photos

  • They're free-license stand-ins from Wikimedia CommonsThey can be used commercially if the photographer is credited, as in the footer. Two of them (the warehouse and the Lagos port photo) are CC BY-SA.
  • Labeled honestlyThe warehouse photo says “not our facility”. The ship and plane photos are labeled “illustrative”, and the footer says the carriers shown aren't affiliated with SG Resources.
  • Replace them with your own over timePhotos of your own cars being loaded, your containers and your Stafford office will build more trust than stock photos. Your Instagram “Automobile” and “Shipping” highlights are a good place to start.
  • Current site imagesThe images on the current site are Wix stock photos (licensed only for use on Wix sites) or AI-looking graphics, so I didn't reuse them.

Colors from the logo

#F36C05#4B0542#6D6D6D

Orange is the globe and “RESOURCES”, plum is the swoosh and “SG”, grey is “INC.” and the tagline. The header uses the original logo, split into the globe and the wordmark so it fits on one line. A vector (SVG) version of the logo would look sharper; ask whoever designed it.

What I found in research

  • Vehicles are the core businessPublic U.S. export records list about 396 export shipments from the Stafford address, mostly vehicles (Toyota, Lexus, vans). This redesign puts vehicles and freight first.
  • Instagram says “Licensed Logistics Hub”The site never says which licenses. Name them (dealer license, FMC/OTI number if you have one) or remove the word “licensed”.
  • Directory listings show a different phone numberSome directories list 254-247-6934. Update those listings or the website so they match.
  • Directories list you as women-ownedIf you hold a certification (WBE, WOSB), show it. It helps on commercial and government jobs.
  • Your Instagram bio includes a China flagIf you source from China, say so in the procurement section. It's a strong selling point.

Sources: Panjiva, Instagram, Buzzfile, ZoomInfo, FindForwarders

What I removed for honesty

  • “Departures board” with live-looking status lightsIt suggested a published schedule. It's now a plain list of routes, noting that dates depend on the carrier.
  • Port codes, “Rail” and “Land”The site doesn't show these services, so I took them out.
  • “30+ years” as a headline statThat's one person's experience, in construction. It now appears only in the Construction section.
  • “Unparalleled”, “premier”, “powerhouse”, “global excellence”Customers read these as filler. I replaced them with plain descriptions of the work.
  • Customs clearance claimsThe site says “assistance”. The vehicle steps now show who does what at the destination.

Please confirm before launch

  • Is air and sea still weekly to all five ports?Onne is a seaport only, so “air” can't apply there.
  • What is the FAAN Complex office?A cargo office at the Lagos airport? Say what customers can do there.
  • Do you have your own warehouse or use a partner's?“Our warehouse facilities” needs to be accurate.
