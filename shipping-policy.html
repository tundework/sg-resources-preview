SG Resources Inc policy: important shipping & liability terms.

Limited liability notice

Goods are handled and/or transported by third-party carriers whose terms and liability limits apply.

No automatic insurance provided

  • We do not provide insurance for your goods.
  • If you wish to insure your shipment, you must obtain coverage through your own private insurance provider.

Customer responsibilities

It is your responsibility to:

  • Declare the nature and value of all items shipped.
